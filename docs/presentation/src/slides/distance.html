Measuring distance with depth

Depth for every pixel

The RealSense camera measures distance per pixel, lined up with the colour image

Median of a small patch

The middle value of a 9 x 9 pixel square at the cone's centre; missing readings are skipped

Backup: depth-only scanner

Fits the ground plane (RANSAC), then anything standing above it and cone-sized is a cone. Median of 15 frames, under 2 mm spread, no colour needed.

The catch

0.31 m

blind spot

Closer than this, the camera can't measure depth. At grabbing range the cone is already too close, so the robot drives in on the cone's image position instead.

Depth runs at 424 x 240 instead of 640 x 480 because the lower resolution shrinks the minimum range. Sanity check on the scanner: the fitted plane's offset equals the camera height, so a wrong number instantly shows the plane was fitted to a wall or a desk.
