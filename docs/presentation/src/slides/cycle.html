Why use AI for the grab?

Our first try was a simple formula from cone pixel to joint angles, fitted on 8 hand-taught grabs. It missed by about 6 cm: 0 of about 15 autonomous grabs worked.

The "Collect cone" button in the web panel runs this whole cycle. Stage 1 runs the ACT model for 15 seconds. Only the grab is learned: the jar sits in a fixed place on the robot, so dropping and returning home are replayed recordings.
