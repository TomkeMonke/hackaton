Why it works

Three ideas behind the model

Action chunking

Planning one tiny move at a time lets errors pile up. Planning 100 at once gives smooth, confident motion.

Pretrained eyes

ResNet18 already learned to see from over a million ImageNet photos. Our 50 demos only tune it to cones.

Grab styles

People never grab the same way twice. The model learns separate styles instead of averaging them into one bad motion.

Pinecone robot - how the software works

Grab styles are handled by a VAE (variational autoencoder), used only during training. At run time the style is fixed to the average, so the arm commits to one consistent way of grabbing. Learning vision from zero with only 50 demos would have been impossible, which is why the pretrained ResNet18 matters.
