The eyes: turns the photo into features - edges, shapes, where the cone is

Transformer

The brain: combines what it sees with where the arm is and decides the motion

ACT came out of Stanford's ALOHA project in 2023. The transformer is the same kind of network as ChatGPT, but it predicts arm positions instead of words. Model size: 512-wide, 4 encoder layers, 1 decoder layer, 8 attention heads.
