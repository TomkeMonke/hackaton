Pinecone robot - how the software works

03

Plain OpenCV, no trained model. The detector sorts blobs so the lowest one in the image comes first: the ground is flat and the camera looks down, so lower in the frame means closer to the robot. Blobs cut off by the top or side edge are marked partial and only used for direction.
