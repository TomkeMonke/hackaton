Plain OpenCV, no trained model. The detector sorts blobs so the lowest one in the image comes first: the ground is flat and the camera looks down, so lower in the frame means closer to the robot. Blobs cut off by the top or side edge are marked partial and only used for direction.

01 - See

Measuring distance with depth

1

Depth for every pixel

The RealSense camera measures distance per pixel, lined up with the colour image

2

Median of a small patch

The middle value of a 9 x 9 pixel square at the cone's centre; missing readings are skipped

Backup: depth-only scanner

Fits the ground plane (RANSAC), then anything standing above it and cone-sized is a cone. Median of 15 frames, under 2 mm spread, no colour needed.

The catch

0.31 m

blind spot

Closer than this, the camera can't measure depth. At grabbing range the cone is already too close, so the robot drives in on the cone's image position instead.

Depth runs at 424 x 240 instead of 640 x 480 because the lower resolution shrinks the minimum range. Sanity check on the scanner: the fitted plane's offset equals the camera height, so a wrong number instantly shows the plane was fitted to a wall or a desk.

Nobody wrote the arm's grab motion by hand. It was learned from demonstrations, which is called imitation learning.

ACT - Action Chunking with Transformers

What the AI model sees and does

Camera image

640 x 480, from the arm

Joint angles

5 joints + gripper

ResNet18

The eyes: turns the photo into features - edges, shapes, where the cone is

Transformer

The brain: combines what it sees with where the arm is and decides the motion

Next 100 moves

About 3.3 s of motion, re-planned after each chunk

Runs on the Raspberry Pi's processor, no GPU needed: about 0.65 s to plan 100 moves.

Pinecone robot - how the software works

08

ACT came out of Stanford's ALOHA project in 2023. The transformer is the same kind of network as ChatGPT, but it predicts arm positions instead of words. Model size: 512-wide, 4 encoder layers, 1 decoder layer, 8 attention heads.

Why it works

Three ideas behind the model

Action chunking

Planning one tiny move at a time lets errors pile up. Planning 100 at once gives smooth, confident motion.

Pretrained eyes

ResNet18 already learned to see from over a million ImageNet photos. Our 50 demos only tune it to cones.

Grab styles

People never grab the same way twice. The model learns separate styles instead of averaging them into one bad motion.

09

Grab styles are handled by a VAE (variational autoencoder), used only during training. At run time the style is fixed to the average, so the arm commits to one consistent way of grabbing. Learning vision from zero with only 50 demos would have been impossible, which is why the pretrained ResNet18 matters.

Home

Why use AI for the grab?

Our first try was a simple formula from cone pixel to joint angles, fitted on 8 hand-taught grabs. It missed by about 6 cm: 0 of about 15 autonomous grabs worked.
